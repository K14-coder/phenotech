# Independent AI review of the atlas links (2026-10-04)

**Reviewer: an AI (Claude), not a human biochemist.** Every record written by this review has `review.by = "ai-review:claude"` and a note that starts with "Independent AI review (Claude), not a human expert." These records are not expert sign-off. The web currently labels every review record "Reviewed by a biochemist". For records where `review.by` starts with `ai-review:` it should say **"Independently reviewed by AI (not a human expert)"**, and it should also show the verdict `needs-human`, which is new. `data/build/report.md` counts these records under "Human-reviewed edges"; `pipeline/build_graph.py` needs the same distinction.

## Scope and method

- Every item in `docs/review/biochem-review.md` (Part 1: 7, Part 2: 2, Part 3: 50), plus the CLAUDE.md flags: the SNAP25 / PMID:25381298 reading; the CBL and SCN8A classes that disagree with G2P; five missing MONDO umbrella ids; the obsolete Dravet id.
- For each link I re-read every cited abstract: stored under `data/raw/**/pubmed`, plus five fetched from PubMed E-utilities (`tool=tasukeru`, no email: 41174879, 41385967, 41475766, 41648160, 41714804). I checked each quote is verbatim (all 59 links' quotes are), whether the source says what the link claims, whether the direction, level and confidence follow `docs/SCHEMA.md`, and whether each source is filed on the right side (supporting vs limiting). Trial claims were checked against the stored ClinicalTrials.gov records, G2P against `data/raw/downloads/g2p_all.csv`, and MONDO ids against `data/raw/downloads/mondo-base.obo`.
- Every quote I added is verbatim from the stored abstract (checked by script, `extracted_by: "ai-review:claude"`), or is an existing verified item copied from another edge.
- Patches are in `data/curated/overrides.json` (`edge_patches`, `node_patches`). Node review notes are under `_review`, which the build does not copy into the graph. `python3 pipeline/build_graph.py` reports "Problems: None".
- The sheet lists item 20 under the old id `therapy:3-4-diaminopyridine|candidate_for|disease:STXBP1`. After the node merge it is `therapy:aminopyridine-presynaptic-boost|candidate_for|disease:STXBP1`, and that is the id reviewed.
- Caveat: an edge patch replaces whole fields. If a fragment later adds evidence to a patched edge, the patched `evidence` / `counter_evidence` list hides it until the patch is updated.
