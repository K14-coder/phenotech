Whole genome sequencing for a rare disease
This booklet is for patients and their family members who
may be offered a whole genome sequencing test for
diagnosis of a rare or inherited condition.
Through the NHS Genomic Medicine Service, whole genome
sequencing is now available for certain conditions where the
scientific evidence shows it can help improve patient care.
Your healthcare professional will provide further information and
you will be able to ask questions before you decide whether to
have this test.
