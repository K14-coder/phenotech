Information for customers in New York state

At this time, we are unable to process any orders from New York State, outside of sponsored testing. Please note Familial Variant Testing (FVT) and Targeted Variant Testing (TVT) are also not available at this time to New York patients, even following use of a sponsored test. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause clinicians or patients in New York State. You can order kits for sponsored testing (i.e. NovoDETECT, My Retina Tracker, and Resonate Program) below. If you have any questions please contact: support.us@blueprintgenetics.com.

Due to the discontinuation of lab services in the US, new kit orders will only be fulfilled for clients who have recently updated their laboratory services agreement (LSA). This updated agreement must acknowledge that your samples will be processed in Helsinki, Finland, and that Blueprint Genetics will invoice you according to our institutional billing practices, in US dollars. Please note that the maximum order quantity for kits is 10.

Kit types
